About CHAT
CHAT is an independent collection of articles, resources, and ideas covering technology, business, science, design, culture, and learning.

We read widely so you can read well. Every article and resource here is chosen because it explains something clearly, holds up over time, or opens a door to a new subject. No noise, no endless feeds.

CHAT is built for people who like to understand how things work, whether that means a new technology, a business idea, or a piece of history.
